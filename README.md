Home Credit LAP Qualification Voice Agent is a prompt-engineered AI voice agent built on Bolna AI. It calls existing customers, identifies itself as an AI assistant, confirms it is speaking to the right person, and presents a pre-approved Loan Against Property offer of up to 75 lakh rupees. It then collects seven eligibility details through free-flowing conversation: property type, ownership, original documents, loan amount, occupation and income mode, market value, and tenure.

The agent applies the business rules exactly. It disqualifies immediately on agricultural property, cash income, missing original documents or an out-of-range tenure. It counter-offers the 75 lakh maximum when the requested amount is higher, and routes existing-loan and EMI-reduction cases to a loan-transfer specialist. It remembers answers given out of order, accepts corrections, handles interruptions and diversions, and never invents interest rates or product facts. Busy customers get a callback booked through Cal.com in a future slot, and a Transfer Call tool serves as a safety exit for customers who insist on a human. The agent supports English and Hindi and keeps its configured gender throughout.

I tested the agent on live calls across all the scenarios in the assignment brief, and every one passed.
